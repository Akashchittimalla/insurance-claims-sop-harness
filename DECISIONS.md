# Design decisions

- **Keep workflow decisions in the backend.** LangGraph routes the turn and named SOP guards control phase changes. Typed proposals make it possible to switch between mock, OpenAI, and Gemini without giving the model authority over identity, claims, or consent. Gemini and OpenAI share an HTTPX transport with fixed endpoints and local schema validation.
- **Use the supplied fixtures as the source of claim facts.** Owner-scoped SQLite queries are enough for this dataset. Use Decimal for recorded amounts, and keep missing amounts distinct from zero. Interpret appeal deadlines as dates in `BUSINESS_TIMEZONE`. The fixtures do not establish an exact cutoff or appeal eligibility.
- **Keep identity inputs local.** Compare aliases within their original field and count distinct approved fields. Raw partial candidates live in process memory and can be lost on restart. Representative requests go to review. National ID is outside the approved factor list.
- **Separate recovery from authorization.** An offer can follow repeated scope or recovery issues. Accepting it records a simulated handoff. Identity review and lockout do not grant access. The persisted source-based verification limit also applies across sessions. People behind a shared gateway can share that limit.
- **Keep browser credentials at the gateway.** Use a session-bound HttpOnly cookie and check the origin of writes. Separately authorize diagnostic requests and keep model keys at the backend. The hosted mock demo disables diagnostics.
- **Record summary choices transactionally.** Bind explicit send or skip to the summary revision and display the masked registered email. SQLite persists message replays and one simulated delivery outcome per session. A real email transport would need delivery reconciliation.
- **Keep the prototype easy to run.** Local Compose uses separate images and a named SQLite volume. Render uses one container for the public frontend and a loopback backend, with temporary SQLite storage. The ZIP includes the setup files, lockfiles, original fixtures and sanitized test evidence.
